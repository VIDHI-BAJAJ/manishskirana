About Manish's

A house built on
one simple belief.

Bring pure, honest ingredients to every home. That belief opened a single shop in 1993, and it still decides what we buy, what we refuse, and whose name we are willing to put on a pack.

Our story

The first counter held a handful of lines — raisins, almonds, jeera — and a rule that nothing went out that we would not cook with ourselves. Word travelled the way it does in a trade town: slowly, then all at once.

Three generations on, the counter has become a catalogue of dried fruits, nuts, seeds, spices and pantry essentials moving in ten-kilo cartons and four-hundred-gram packs. The rule has not moved at all.

Philosophy

We would rather lose an order than send out something we would not put in our own kitchen.

Buy at origin

Figs and dates from the Gulf, hazelnuts from Türkiye, cashews from Vietnam and India — chosen crop by crop, not by catalogue.

Grade by hand

Size, colour and softness are judged by people who have judged them for decades. Machines help; they do not decide.

Say what is inside

Origin, grade and packing printed plainly. A buyer should never have to ask what they are actually getting.
