Since 1982, Manish’s has been built around a simple belief that quality products, dependable service, and lasting relationships are the foundation of a trusted business.

Our story

Radha Sarweshwar & Co. was established in 1982 by Late Sri Balkishen Rathi at Begum Bazaar, Hyderabad, beginning as a humble dry fruits and kirana store with a simple vision to provide customers with quality products and dependable service.

Over the years, the business expanded beyond traditional retail and pioneered product packaging under its own brand, Manish’s Kirana & Dry Fruit Packing. What began with careful hand-packing gradually evolved into modern packaging systems, adapting to changing technology and customer needs.
