Our story

A Legacy Of Purity.

Radha Sarweshwar & Co. began in 1982 at Begum Bazaar, Hyderabad, as a humble dry fruits and kirana store founded by Late Sri Balkishen Rathi.

With a simple vision of providing quality products and dependable service, the business grew under the dedicated efforts of Sri Ghanshyam Das Rathi and Sri Gopal Rathi.

Over the years, Manish’s evolved from careful hand-packing to modern packaging systems, combining traditional values with changing technology and consumer needs.
